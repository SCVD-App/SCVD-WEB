Your information

Your details stay on your phone

Names, birthdays and charts are stored on your device, and all the maths runs right there too. There are no accounts and no database of users, so there’s nothing of yours on our side to lose, leak or sell.

The only things that leave your device: the place name you type (to find its coordinates), and if you buy Pro, the email you paid with (so we can resend your code if you lose it). No ads. No subscriptions.

Tip: use Back up your charts in Settings to save a copy you can load on a new phone.

Good questions
I don’t know my birth time. Can I still use it?

Yes. Your Sun sign, planets, numbers and Chinese animal don’t need a time. Your Rising sign and houses do, because they change every few minutes, so Duology leaves those out and tells you why. Find your time later (a birth certificate, or Mum’s memory)? Just edit your chart.

Is this the real sky, or made-up stuff?

The planet positions are calculated from real astronomy for your exact date, time and place. The meanings are the traditional readings astrologers and numerologists use, and Duology explains where each one comes from. It’s for reflection and entertainment, not predictions.

Which numerology does it use?

Pythagorean by default, with Chaldean one tap away. Master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are kept or reduced, your choice.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Every pass is a one-off payment. The 30-day and 12-month passes simply stop when they end. Lifetime never ends.

Can I use it on my phone and my laptop?

Yes. Duology runs in any modern browser. Your Pro code works on any device: paste it into Settings → Have a code? To move your charts, use the backup file.
